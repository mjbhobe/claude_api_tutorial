# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Training Athlete

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete training for a marathon with vegetarian dietary restrictions:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (110g), Fat (85g), Carbs (480g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), ground flaxseed (15g), and soy milk (240ml).
    * Protein: 12g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 85g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Whole wheat bread (60g) with peanut butter (30g) and honey (20g).
    * Protein: 10g, Fat: 16g, Carbs: 55g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Chickpea and vegetable curry with chickpeas (150g cooked), onions (80g), tomatoes (100g), spinach (100g), and coconut milk (100ml). Served with brown rice (150g cooked).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 85g

* **Pre-Workout Snack (3:30 PM):** Energy bar (50g) made with oats and dates, plus sports drink (500ml) with electrolytes and 30g carbohydrates.
    * Protein: 6g, Fat: 5g, Carbs: 60g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Grilled tofu steak (200g) with sweet potato (250g baked), steamed broccoli (150g), and olive oil (15ml).
    * Protein: 28g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 70g

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM):** Greek yogurt (200g, non-fat) with granola (40g) and berries (80g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 6g, Carbs: 50g

**Hydration Note:** Drink 2.5-3 liters of water throughout the day, increasing intake on training days.

This meal plan emphasizes high carbohydrate intake for marathon endurance, includes adequate plant-based protein sources, and provides sustained energy for long-distance training.
