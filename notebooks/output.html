# Marathon Race Day Nutrition Strategy

## Pre-Race Carb Loading (3-7 days before)

**Daily intake:** 8-12g carbs/kg body weight (544-816g for this person)
- **Pasta, rice, potatoes:** 3-4 large servings daily
- **Bread & cereals:** 6-8 slices whole grain bread
- **Fruits:** Bananas, dates, berries (400-500g)
- **Reduce fiber & fat** to minimize GI distress

---

## Race Day Nutrition (3000+ calories)

### **2-3 hours before start:**
- 200-300g carbs + moderate protein, low fat/fiber
- *Example: 2 bagels with peanut butter, banana, sports drink*
- Hydrate: 400-600ml water

### **During race (carbs every 45-60 min):**
| Time | Carb Source | Amount | Hydration |
|------|-------------|--------|-----------|
| 0-45 min | Sports drink | 200-300ml | Plain water |
| 45-90 min | Energy gel + water | 25-30g carbs | 200ml water |
| Every 45 min | Sports drink OR gels | 30-60g carbs/hour | 400-800ml/hour |

**Carb options per hour:**
- 1-1.5 liters sports drink (6-8%), OR
- 2-3 energy gels + water, OR
- Energy bars + electrolyte drink

### **Hydration strategy:**
- **Drink to thirst** but aim for: 400-800ml/hour (adjust for heat/sweat rate)
- **Electrolytes:** 500-700mg sodium per hour
- **Avoid:** Hyponatremia (over-hydrating plain water)

### **Post-race (recovery):**
- 1.2g carbs/kg within 30 min → 82g carbs
- *Example: Chocolate milk, banana, sports drink*

---

## Key Tips
✓ **Test everything** during training runs  
✓ **Sodium aids hydration** retention  
✓ **Individual sweat rates vary**—practice your plan  
✓ **Mix carb sources** (drinks + gels) for GI comfort
